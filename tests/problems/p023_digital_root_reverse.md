# Digital Root and Mirror

The digital root of a positive integer is obtained by repeatedly replacing the number with the sum of its decimal digits until a single digit remains. The mirror of a number is the number written with its digits in reverse order, with leading zeros removed. For each of T numbers print its digital root and its mirror.

### Input
The first line contains T (1 ≤ T ≤ 10^4). Each of the following T lines contains one integer n (1 ≤ n ≤ 10^18).

### Output
For each number print the digital root and the mirror separated by a space.

### Example 1
Input:
3
12345
100
9
Output:
6 54321
1 1
9 9

### Example 2
Input:
2
999999999999999999
10
Output:
9 999999999999999999
1 1
